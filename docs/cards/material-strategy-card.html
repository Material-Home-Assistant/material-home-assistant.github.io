🧭 Material Strategy Card ​

NOTE

✨ Available on 💎 Ultimate Version Only

✨ Overview ​

The Material Strategy Card (custom:material-strategy-card) brings the intelligent, auto-generated sections of the Material Dashboard Card into any custom Lovelace view or dashboard layout. Instead of manually adding and maintaining dozens of individual cards, you can drop a single material-strategy-card configured for one of the 18 thematic sections—and optionally a specific Home Assistant Area—to automatically render Material 3 Expressive cards sorted, styled, and kept in sync with your entities.

Empty sections automatically collapse to zero height in View Mode (while displaying an informative placeholder in Edit Mode), and every section exposes the underlying configuration (cards_config) of its generated Material cards.

☀️ Light Mode

🌙 Dark Mode

✨ Key Features ​

  • 🗂️ 18 Dedicated Sections: Automatically discovers and renders cards for Areas, Security Favorites, Favorites, Lights, Covers, Climate, Media Players, Security, Switches, Energy, Ambient Sensors, Devices, Actions, Events, Controls, Vacuums, Infrastructure, and Buttons.
  • 🏠 Area-Scoped or Cross-Area Grouped: Render entities for a single area, or enable show_area_group: true on Lights, Climate, Media Players, and Actions to group every matching entity across all Home Assistant areas (including unassigned entities).
  • 🏷️ Smart Label Filtering & Disabled Entity Exclusion: Automatically ignores hidden or disabled entities/devices and skips anything tagged with hidden_label.
  • 🎛️ Full Underlying Card Exposure (cards_config): Pass native configuration and style options directly to the generated Material cards (slider percentages, extended climate/media/alarm cards, inline graph metrics, template styling, chevron visibility).
  • ➕ Additional Cards Support (cards): Mix custom Lovelace cards inside area-scoped sections—merged and sorted automatically alongside auto-generated cards (see Additional Cards Support).
  • 📐 Responsive 24-Column Container Grid: Adapts card spans (full, 12, 8, 6 columns) automatically across mobile (< 400px), tablet (400px–600px), and desktop (> 600px) container widths.
  • 🧹 Zero-Space Empty Collapse & Smart Bottom Spacing: Empty sections collapse completely in View Mode leaving zero orphan whitespace, and trailing bottom spacers are suppressed automatically on mobile when placed last in a view section.

Areas ​

Section: areas · Config Key: area_config · Scope: Global

Displayed Devices & Behavior ​

Displays a Home Assistant Areas overview using every area registered in hass.areas, sorted alphabetically. Each room renders as a custom:material-template-card displaying the area's icon (falling back to mdi:door-closed if none is set). When enabled, active lighting or security activity (light on, or motion / occupancy / presence / door / window / opening / vibration active) highlights the room tile with Material active container styling (area-active), while inline room temperature and humidity readings appear directly in the subtitle. Tapping an area navigates to ./<area_id> or a custom route mapped in area_pages.

Security Favorites ​

Section: security_favorites · Config Key: security_favourites_config · Scope: Global

Displayed Devices & Behavior ​

Scans your entire Home Assistant instance for security-related entities or devices tagged with security_label_favorite (falling back to "preferiti" if unset). Matching items include alarm_control_panel.*, lock.*, camera.*, and sensor / binary_sensor entities whose device_class is motion, occupancy, presence, door, window, opening, vibration, smoke, gas, safety, or tamper. Alarm panels are always sorted first, followed alphabetically by remaining items. When show_extended_cards is true, alarm panels render responsive desktop (extended) and mobile (compact) custom:material-alarm-card pairs; all other security items use custom:material-template-card.

Favorites ​

Section: favorites · Config Key: favorites_config · Scope: Global

Displayed Devices & Behavior ​

Collects all non-security entities and devices carrying favorites_label (fallback "preferiti"), across every room. Security entities (alarm_control_panel, lock, camera, security device_class sensors) are excluded automatically so they do not duplicate Security Favorites. Results are prioritized cleanly: media_player entities first, climate entities second, then all other domains alphabetically—generating material-media-card / material-climate-card (extended desktop 12-column spans when show_extended_cards: true), material-button-card, material-slider-card (light / cover), or material-template-card (chevrons automatically disabled for switch, input_boolean, and automation).

Lights ​

Section: lights · Config Key: lights_config / grouped_lights_config · Scope: Single Area or Grouped (show_area_group: true)

Displayed Devices & Behavior ​

Collects every light.* entity in the chosen area (or grouped by every Home Assistant area plus unassigned lights when show_area_group: true). Each light is rendered as a custom:material-slider-card (control_type: light) supporting brightness percentage display and state labels. When show_lights_card is enabled, a master custom:material-lights-card (All On / All Off pill controls) appears above the individual light sliders—scoped to the room in single-area mode, or controlling all house lights in grouped mode. In single-area mode, setting primary_entity adds an inline status badge inside the section header.

Climate ​

Section: climate · Config Key: climate_config / grouped_climate_config · Scope: Single Area or Grouped (show_area_group: true)

Displayed Devices & Behavior ​

Combines HVAC equipment and ambient comfort sensors into a single cohesive block (for a chosen area, or grouped across all areas + unassigned entities when show_area_group: true):

  1. HVAC & Airflow (climate.*, humidifier.*, water_heater.*, fan.*): Rendered as interactive custom:material-climate-card widgets when show_extended_cards: true, or compact custom:material-button-card (control_type: thermometer) tiles when false. Extended cards are always sorted above compact cards.
  2. Ambient Sensors (sensor.* with device_class: temperature or humidity): Rendered directly below the HVAC cards as either custom:material-template-card (sensor_card_type: "template") or custom:material-graph-card (sensor_card_type: "graph").

Media Players ​

Section: media · Config Key: media_config / grouped_media_config · Scope: Single Area or Grouped (show_area_group: true)

Displayed Devices & Behavior ​

Collects all media_player.* entities (smart TVs, Chromecasts, smart speakers, AV receivers) in the selected area or grouped across every area. Players can render as compact custom:material-button-card tiles or full artwork/playback custom:material-media-card cards. With show_extended_cards_on_play: true (default in grouped mode), idle/off devices stay compact and automatically expand into full media cards whenever their state is playing, paused, or idle. Extended cards are always sorted at the top, followed alphabetically by compact cards.

Energy ​

Section: energy · Config Key: energy_config · Scope: Single Area

Displayed Devices & Behavior ​

Collects area sensor.* entities matching device_class energy, power, voltage, current, battery, or power_factor. Cards render either as custom:material-template-card (card_type: "template") or inline sparkline custom:material-graph-card (card_type: "graph"). By setting graph_device_classes, you can render interactive history graphs exclusively for specific classes (for example power and battery) while keeping others as template cards. Low-battery sensors (<= 15%) in template mode automatically highlight using Material 3 error/alert colors.

Section: device · Config Key: devices_config · Scope: Single Area

Additional Cards Support ​

Beyond auto-discovered Home Assistant entities, the Material Strategy Card supports injecting your own custom Lovelace cards inside the section using the root cards list.

Where Additional Cards Are Supported ​

  • Supported Sections: Available on all 15 area-scoped sections (lights, cover, climate, media, security, switches, energy, sensor, device, actions, events, controls, vacuums, infrastructure, buttons) when show_area_group is false.
  • Not Supported: Global sections (areas, favorites, security_favorites) and grouped cross-area sections (show_area_group: true) do not accept cards, and the Visual Editor hides the Cards panel automatically in those modes.

How Additional Cards Are Merged & Rendered ​

  1. Visual Editor or YAML: Add any native Home Assistant or custom Material card either visually via the Cards stack editor panel or under the root cards: key in YAML.
  2. Alphabetical Integration: Custom cards are combined with auto-generated section cards and sorted alphabetically by each card's name, title, or entity field (mergeAndSortCards).
    • In lights, custom cards are merged and sorted among the individual light sliders below the master All On / All Off material-lights-card.
    • In climate, custom cards are merged and sorted among the ambient temperature/humidity sensor cards (after HVAC/climate cards).
    • In media, extended material-media-card instances remain sorted at the top while compact cards and custom cards sort alphabetically below them.
  3. Always Visible When Populated: If the selected area has zero auto-discovered native entities for that section, providing at least one entry in cards still renders the section title, your custom cards, and bottom spacing instead of collapsing to empty.
  4. Configuration Isolation: cards_config applies strictly to auto-generated cards; each card inside cards: retains its full native configuration and responsive grid_options (columns: 6 | 8 | 12 | full, rows).
yaml
type: custom:material-strategy-card
section: energy
area: utility_room
energy_config:
  section_title: Power & Solar
  card_type: graph
  graph_device_classes:
    - power
  cards_config:
    hours_to_show: 24
    show_shavron_icon: true
cards:
  - type: custom:material-template-card
    entity: sensor.daily_solar_yield
    name: Daily Solar
    icon: mdi:solar-power
    show_shavron_icon: true
