Privacy Policy ​

INFO

Last Updated: 06 May 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Material Home Assistant Development Team ("we", "our"), based in Italy, collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our service, website, and Home Assistant integrations.

1. Data Controller ​

The data controller is the development team of Material Home Assistant, located in Italy. You can reach us for any privacy-related inquiries at support@materialhomeassistant.com.

2. Information Collected ​

We follow a "data minimization" principle, collecting only what is strictly necessary to manage your license:

  • License & Account Data: Email Address, Secret Key, and License Token. These are used to verify your subscription and link it to your instance.
  • No Technical Tracking: We do not log your IP address. Our website does not use cookies for tracking, profiling, or analytics. Any cookies required for payment processing are managed exclusively by Stripe on their own domain.

3. What We DO NOT Collect (Local Privacy) ​

Material Home Assistant is designed to respect your home's privacy. We do not access, collect, or store:

  • Data from your sensors, cameras, or smart devices.
  • Floor plans, names of rooms, or geographical location of your home.
  • Your daily habits, automation logs, or usage history.

All your smart home telemetry remains strictly local to your Home Assistant instance and is never transmitted to our servers.

4. Purposes and Legal Basis ​

We process your data based on the following legal grounds:

  • Performance of a Contract: To provide the Service, manage your account, and validate your "Premium" or "Pro" license.
  • Legal Obligations: To comply with tax regulations or requests from judicial authorities.

5. Payment Security and Stripe ​

We rely on Stripe, Inc. for financial transactions.

  • Direct Redirect: Payments are handled via a secure redirect to Stripe's platform.
  • No Sensitive Data Storage: Material Home Assistant never sees or stores your credit card numbers, CVC codes, or expiration dates.
  • For more details, please consult Stripe's Privacy Policy.

6. Data Retention and Security ​

  • Retention: Your license data is kept as long as your account is active or as required by law for tax purposes.
  • Security: We adopt industry-standard technical measures to protect your email and license tokens from unauthorized access.

7. Third-Party Data Sharing ​

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties for commercial or marketing purposes. Data is only shared with Stripe to process payments or if required by Italian/EU law.

8. Your Rights (GDPR) ​

Under the GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access, rectify, or request the erasure of your personal data.
  • Object to or restrict the processing of your data.
  • Request data portability.

To exercise these rights, please contact us via email.

9. Changes to this Policy ​

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our service or legal requirements. Users will be notified of significant changes through our website or via email.

10. Contacts ​

For questions regarding your privacy:
