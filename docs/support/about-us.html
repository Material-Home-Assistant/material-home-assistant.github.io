Material Home Assistant

Welcome to the Material Home Assistant organization! ✨
We are a team of passionate developers creating custom integrations and components for Home Assistant,
with a focus on modern design, usability, and seamless integration.

👨‍💻 Who We Are ​

We are the founders and lead developers of this project. Our mission is to enhance the Home Assistant user experience through powerful and aesthetically pleasing components.

Giovanni Lamarmora

Developer & Owner

Francesco Vitale

Developer & Owner

🚀 Our Main Projects ​

Here is an overview of our flagship projects. Click to learn more!

🎨 Material Home Assistant Integration

A suite of Lovelace components that brings the Google Home app experience directly into Home Assistant, implementing Material You design principles for a sleek and modern interface.

🐙 Octopus Energy Adapter

A "bridge" integration that solves the data delay from Octopus Energy Italy, importing historical consumption data into Home Assistant's Energy Panel for accurate and timely analysis.
